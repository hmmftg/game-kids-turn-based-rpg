# PR S Decision Memo — template

Fill this in **after** the PR R observation sessions
([USABILITY-PROTOCOL.md](./USABILITY-PROTOCOL.md) +
[observation sheets](./usability-observation-sheet.md)). Its purpose: turn
observations into **decisions**, not comments — a prioritized backlog that says
what ships next and what must not.

Rules:

- Every claim cites a session artifact (sheet, clip timestamp, tally) — no
  impressions.
- A finding enters the backlog only via the decision table; exceptions are
  argued in §5, not smuggled in.
- "Not a problem" findings are listed too — exploration diversity is the design
  working, and we want the record of what we deliberately did _not_ fix.
